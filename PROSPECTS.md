## Dynamic Pilates (Manly) — demo in progress

**Status (23 Sep):** brief registered, not yet built against. An earlier,
much less detailed demo (`dynamic-pilates-via-demo.html`) was built
in-session before this real brief existed — that file lived in ephemeral
scratchpad and likely didn't survive the container. Treat this brief as the
authoritative source and rebuild from it rather than trying to recover that
earlier file. Being built alongside Otherwise — check with Virginie on
sequencing before assuming which comes first.

**Purpose:** a pre-built demo app to send to Gemma Sadler (founder/owner).
She currently uses Mindbody with a Mindbody-branded app. Goal: show her what
her studio looks like on VIA so switching feels effortless and obvious. Demo
only — no real payments or live booking needed. The wow moment is the first
screen: premium and calm, her name and studio front and centre, class
schedule visible immediately. It needs to look better than her existing
Mindbody-branded App Store app.

credentials and community. Not loud or trendy — authoritative and warm.

if it gets built for either client.
