1

First time here

Create your account

On the login screen, tap Sign up.

  1. Enter your full name, email, and a password (at least 6 characters).
  2. Add your phone number and birthday — instructors use these to reach you and to celebrate with you.
  3. Tap Create Account and you're straight into the app.
Forgotten your password later? On the login screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and follow the link that arrives.
2

The main event

Browse & book a class

Tap Classes in the menu. Filter by Alexandria, Manly, or Special Classes if you're after something specific.

  1. Open a class to see the time, location and who's teaching it.
  2. If you already have an active pass, tap Book · Use Pass — it deducts one credit automatically.
  3. No pass yet? Tap Book · Casual to pay for just that one class, or More options ↓ if a longer session length is offered.
  4. You'll land on your dashboard showing You're booked! once payment goes through.

  1. Got a discount code? Type it into Enter code and tap Apply — do this before tapping Buy on a pass, not after. A code only discounts the purchase you make while it's applied; it can't be used retroactively on something you've already bought.
  2. Tap Buy under whichever pass suits you.
  3. Complete payment — you'll see a Purchase successful! popup with a button straight to Classes.
  4. Buying a pass doesn't book a class by itself — head to Classes and tap Book · Use Pass when you're ready.
Have more than one pass at once (say, a 5-class pack and a leftover casual credit)? Both show on the Classes page — booking always uses whichever one expires soonest first.
4

Plans changed

Cancel a booking

From either your Dashboard or the Classes page, find the class you're booked into.

  1. Tap Cancel booking (or Cancel on the Classes page).
  2. Confirm — cancelling frees your spot and, if it's outside 24 hours of the class, returns your pass credit.
Cancelling inside 24 hours of the class doesn't return the credit — see the cancellation policy below.

  2. Tap Download to save a copy to your device for offline practice.

11

Worth knowing before you book

Cancellation policy

Class packages are non-refundable, and valid only for their stated duration unless BYLA approves an exception.

  1. Cancel at least 24 hours before class and your credit is returned.
  2. Cancel later than that, or don't show up, and the credit isn't refunded or rescheduled.
  3. Can't make it? You can transfer your spot to someone else — just let BYLA know who's coming in your place.
Full policy is always linked at the bottom of your booking confirmation email.
