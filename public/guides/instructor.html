Do this first, before anything else

Set your own password

You were given a temporary password to get started. Replace it with one only you know.

  1. If you're logged in, sign out first. On the login screen, tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter the email address your account was created with, then tap Send reset link.
  3. Open the email from BYLA and click the reset link inside it.
  4. Type your new password into New password and again into Confirm password, then save.
  5. Log back in with your email and the new password.
Don't see the email within a few minutes? Check spam/junk before asking Majo to resend anything.
2

Worth knowing before you dive in

What you can (and can't) do

Your account has everything you need to run classes day-to-day. A short list of things are admin-only.

You can

  • Create, edit and take attendance on any class
  • Delete classes you created yourself
  • Assign yourself to teach a class
  • Manage members, passes and walk-ins
  • Post BYLA News, add playlists and videos
  • Edit your own bio and photo

Admin only

  • Revenue & attendance reports
  • Discount codes
  • Deleting a class you didn't create
  • Editing another instructor's bio
3

On the day of class

Take the roll

From the app's top menu, tap Dashboard — this opens your instructor dashboard on the Classes tab by default.

  1. Find the class in your Upcoming list. If it's today, its button already says Take Roll — otherwise it says Attendance. Tap it either way.
  2. You'll see everyone booked in. Tick each person off as they arrive.
  3. Left the tab open overnight or came back the next day? Just reopen the class from Upcoming and pick up where you left off — nothing is lost.
4

Someone shows up without a booking

Add a walk-in or a client to the roll

From inside the roll for that class, tap + Add to Roll.

  1. Choose Existing Member if they already have an account, or Walk-in (no account) if they don't.
  2. Existing Member: pick their name, then choose which pass to deduct from — or leave it as Complimentary / Casual (paid on spot) if they're not using a pass.
  3. Walk-in: type their name, set how they're paying, and optionally deduct the class from an existing member's pass if that's how they're covering it (e.g. a member bringing a friend).
  4. Tap Add to Roll to confirm.
5

After a good class

Send review emails

On the Classes tab, tap Send Review Emails. Want to send to a wider group, or catch up later? Use Marketing > Review Requests instead — see section 14.

  1. Pick the class you want to send from.
  2. Select which members to include — everyone's ticked by default, untick anyone you'd rather skip.
  3. Preview the email, then send.
6

Music for members

Add a playlist

Open the Playlists tab and tap + Add Playlist.

  1. Paste the Spotify playlist link.
  2. Give it a title and, if you like, a short description.
  3. Tap Add Playlist — it now shows up for members under Playlists.
7

Class recordings

Upload a video

Open the Videos tab and tap + Add Videos. You can select more than one file at once — they'll upload one after another.

  1. Choose the video file(s) from your phone or computer.
  2. Add a title (and description if you want one) — with multiple files, the same title/description applies to all of them.
  3. Link it to a specific class if relevant, and choose whether it's visible to all members or only those who booked.
  4. Tap Add Video (or Add Videos for several) and wait for the upload to finish.
To watch it back, that's not on this dashboard — open the main menu (tap ☰ top right) and go to Videos. That's the same place members go to watch it.
If it's visible to all members, everyone with push notifications turned on gets an alert as soon as you publish it.
8

Your public profile

Update your bio & title

This is what shows on BYLA's public Instructors page. You can only edit your own — not the other instructors'.

  1. Open the Instructors tab on your dashboard.
  2. Find your own row and tap Edit.
  3. Update your Title (e.g. "Instructor") and Bio, then save.
You can also update your name, phone, birthday and photo from My Profile in the main menu — and switch on Push Notifications there too, so you get an alert for new videos, chat messages, and news, same as members.
Not getting notified? The toggle is tied to your device, not your account — if you've ever switched between two accounts on the same phone, only the most recent one stays subscribed, and the toggle can keep showing "on" even after it's gone stale. Fix: go to My Profile on the affected device and switch Push Notifications off, then on again.
9

One-off class

Add a class

On the Classes tab, tap + New Class.

  1. Fill in the title, date and time.
  2. Tap the Alexandria or Manly button to auto-fill the location and standard description — leave the special-class box unticked and this fills in automatically.
  3. Set price, capacity and duration.
  4. If it's a one-off pop-up, collab, or video shoot, tick special class and give it a label — the standard description won't auto-fill for these, so write your own.
  5. Tap Create Class.
Made a mistake after publishing? Every class has an Edit button — no need to delete and start over.
10

Recurring weekly classes

Bulk create classes

On the Classes tab, tap + Bulk Create. This fills in every upcoming week on a chosen day that doesn't already have a class.

  1. Give it a title and pick the day of the week and time.
  2. Choose how far ahead to create classes ("Create classes until").
  3. Tap Alexandria or Manly to fill location and description, then set price, capacity and duration.
  4. Tap Create to generate the batch.
11

So your name shows on the class

Put yourself on a class

A new class doesn't automatically say who's teaching it. Under the date on any class, there's an italic link — it reads Assign instructor if nobody's set yet, or w/ Name if someone already is.

  1. Tap that link.
  2. In the Instructor dropdown, you'll only see your own name — that's expected, instructors can only assign themselves, not each other.
  3. Select yourself and tap Save. It now shows w/ Your Name on the class, both on your dashboard and the public Classes page.
Co-teaching with someone? A second, optional dropdown lets whoever's already assigned add a second name — ask Majo or Virginie to add the other instructor if you're setting it up for the first time. Teaching alongside someone who doesn't have a BYLA account at all — a guest teacher? Type their name into the Guest instructor field instead. The class then shows to members as "w/ You x Guest Name".
12

Announcements members see on their dashboard

Post to BYLA News

Open the BYLA News tab and tap + New Post.

  1. Write a title and the announcement text.
  2. Pick a category — general, location update, event, or routine.
  3. Add a photo if it helps, and pin it if it's important enough to stay at the top.
  4. Post it — members see it on their dashboard as soon as they open the app, and anyone with push notifications on gets an alert straight away.
Door codes: when Manly's monthly door code changes, this is where it gets posted so members can find it.
13

Onboarding a new client

Add a member through the dashboard

Open the Members tab and tap + Invite Member.

  1. Enter their name, email and phone number.
  2. Save — they'll get an email to set up their own login.
  3. Adding several people at once? Use Import CSV instead of adding them one by one.
14

Reaching out to members

Marketing tools

Open Marketing from the main menu. Everyone gets two of its tabs — the others (Reviews, Discounts, Birthdays, Reminders) are admin-only and won't appear on your dashboard.

  1. Review Requests — the same first-timers/any-member review-email tool from section 5, in one place for every class.
  2. Message a Segment — pick a group (no active pass, inactive 3+ weeks, or all members) and send them a one-off email.
First-timers also get a review-request email automatically, about an hour after their first class ends — you don't have to remember to send one. It's the same email as the manual "First-timers by class" option, just on a timer. An admin can pause this from Marketing > Reminders.
Reviews, Discounts, Birthdays and Reminders sit alongside these on the same page but are admin-only — ask Majo if you need something changed there.
